KILN HALLOWEEN - FREE SAMPLER
=============================

A free taste of KILN HALLOWEEN - 16x16 Haunted Graveyard Pack, by bitkiln.

INCLUDED
--------
  - The complete Haunted Graveyard tileset: 16 floors, 47-tile autotile hedges, 47-tile dirt path,
    47-tile animated ectoplasm, 47-tile open graves, hedge variants, door + iron gate animations,
    stairs, cellar door (with Godot 4 TileSet terrains and a Tiled tileset with a terrain set)
  - 15 props, 4 characters (1 hero, 3 monsters, all animated), 12 item icons, UI basics
  - Godot 4 demo scene (copy the kiln_halloween folder into your project, open godot/demo_halloween.tscn, F6)
  - Tiled example map

The full pack adds the Haunted Manor, 71 props, 19 characters (trick-or-treaters, NPCs,
monsters and 2 bosses), 43 items, the full Halloween UI kit and 12 effects.
Find it on the same itch.io creator page.
